## Why a coregistration check was added
The GM Dice printed on the overlays compares the participant's normalised T1 grey matter with the TPM. It checks the T1 normalisation, not the EPI-to-T1 coregistration, and it cannot see a coregistration error: the 24-h run of sub-67823 scored 0.76 and the runs of sub-67903 scored 0.74–0.76, although their EPI was misplaced. GM Dice is therefore not used as evidence below.

While the overlays were being reviewed, the 30-minute slab of sub-67823 was visibly displaced. Every run's coregistration was then checked with the three measures below. They locate misalignment for the visual judgement; they are not a new exclusion rule.
1. **Size of the correction.** This is the shift at the slab centre and the rotation that SPM coregistration applied relative to the scanner coordinates.
   - The pipeline leaves the T1 headers unchanged, and the EPI and T1 of a session share scanner coordinates, so a correct correction is small.
   - Reference values: in the whole-brain arm, all 60 modelled runs are within 2.9 mm and 3.7°. In the small-FOV arm, the runs not discussed below are within 4.3 mm.
2. **Agreement with the learning run.** All runs of session 1 have the same slab placement in scanner coordinates and were acquired in one sitting. Their corrections should therefore agree.
3. **Agreement between the EPI and the T1 CSF.** CSF is bright in T2*-weighted EPI. The measure is the Spearman ρ between the mean EPI and the same-session T1 CSF probability, computed under the SPM coregistration and under the scanner coordinates, in two regions:
   - the whole slab (`coreg_slab_rho.txt`);
   - a medial-temporal box, MNI |x| 15–45 mm, y −45 to −5 mm and z −32 to 0 mm, mapped through the participant's inverse deformation (`coreg_mtl_rho.tsv`).

Tables: `coreg_check.tsv`, `coreg_slab_rho.txt` and `coreg_mtl_rho.tsv`, all in `results/qc_overlays_v2/`.

## Consequences
- **RQ2 (small-FOV arm).** 16 participants, against 20 before the QC decisions. The four-trial rule then applies per contrast.
- **RQ3 (whole-brain arm).** All 20 participants.
- **RQ1 (behaviour, H1).** All 40 participants. This rule concerns the neural analyses only.
- **No repair of coregistration in the primary analysis.** The registered remedy for misalignment is exclusion.
  - Halil asked for a repaired rerun as an unregistered sensitivity analysis.
  - It is specified in `qc_records/SENSITIVITY_COREG_PLAN.md` before any H2 value is read, and it is written to a separate output folder.
- **Observation for the paper.** The coregistration step is the authors' own code (commit e257c25), which we ran unchanged apart from paths. Whether the authors' published C1 analysis contains the same misregistrations is not known; their outputs are not public.
